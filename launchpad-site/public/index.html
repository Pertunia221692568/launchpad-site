    Why LaunchPad.dev exists

    Career acceleration, built for South African ICT students

    LaunchPad.dev is a career platform where students in Application Development, Software Engineering and related ICT diplomas get the three things job-hunting usually lacks: a CV an ATS will actually pass, real interview practice, and a mentor who's already done the job you're applying for.

    How it works

    1

    Sign up

    Create a free student account in under a minute — or sign up as a mentor to start building your public profile.

    2

    Build your CV

    Upload your existing CV for an AI-powered ATS review, or build one from scratch with our guided CV Builder.

    3

    Practice for real

    Run actual code against real test cases in the Interview Arena — coding questions, not just theory.

    4

    Connect with a mentor

    Browse mentors by role and location, book a 1:1 session, and get their direct contact details to keep in touch.

    Who it's for

    Students

    If you're studying towards an ICT diploma or degree and want your CV, interview skills and network to be job-ready before you graduate — not after — this is built for you.

    Mentors

    If you're already working in tech and want to give back — reviewing CVs, running mock interviews, or just being someone a student can message with questions — you can build a public profile and take bookings here.

    What you get

    AI CV Review

    ATS compatibility, formatting and readability scoring, with section-by-section, prioritised recommendations.

    Interview Arena

    Coding challenges across Easy, Medium and Hard, in JavaScript, Python or Java, with your code actually run against test cases.

    Real Mentors

    Search by skill or location, read reviews from other students, book sessions, and get direct contact details.

    Admin panel

    Only accounts listed in ADMIN_EMAILS can see this page. Everything here is read from your MySQL database.
